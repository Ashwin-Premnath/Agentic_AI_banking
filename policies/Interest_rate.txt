Term Deposit Rate Structure (Generic)
This structure represents how the bank break down rates by tenure. 

7 days to 29 days: 3.00%
30 days to 45 days: 3.25%
46 days to 90 days: 4.25%
91 days to 180 days: 4.50%
181 days to 270 days: 5.50%
1 Year: 6.25%
15 Months: 6.60%
3 Years: 6.75%
5 Years to 10 Years: 6.40% 

Important Notes on Deposits

Senior Citizens: Generally receive an additional 0.50% to 0.75% p.a. over the normal rate.

Compounding: Interest is mostly compounded quarterly.

Premature Withdrawal: Usually penalized by a 0.50% to 1% reduction in the interest rate.

Deposit Insurance: FDs with banks are insured up to ₹5 Lakh per depositor by DICGC.

Special Deposit: Some banks offer higher rates for specific tenures (e.g., 450 days or 999 days).
