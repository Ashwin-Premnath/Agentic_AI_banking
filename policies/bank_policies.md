# Global Digital Bank - Company Policies & FAQ

## 1. Account Opening
* Customers must be 18 years or older.
* A valid government ID and proof of address are required (KYC).
* Minimum initial deposit for a Savings Account is $100.

## 2. Interest Rates
* Savings Account: 4.5% APY
* Fixed Deposit (1 Year): 6.0% APY
* Fixed Deposit (3 Years): 7.5% APY
* Rates are subject to change based on market conditions.

## 3. Transaction Limits
* Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $1,000.
* Daily online transfer limit: $10,000.
* International transfers may take 3-5 business days.

## 4. Documentation Policy (KYC)
* Users are required to upload a clear image of their Passport, National ID, or Driver's License.
* Document verification usually takes 24-48 hours.

## 5. Security & Isolation
* We use end-to-end encryption for all transactions.
* User data is isolated and only accessible via secure session authentication.

## FAQ
**Q: How do I reset my PIN?**
A: You can reset your PIN through the mobile app or visit any branch.

**Q: Are there monthly maintenance fees?**
A: No, our standard savings accounts have zero maintenance fees if a minimum balance of $100 is maintained.

**Q: How do I close my account?**
A: Please contact our support team or visit a branch with your ID for account closure.
